Adapted from gracedishes.com. This is the dinner that turns a plain salmon fillet into something special — a creamy, sweet-and-spicy bang bang glaze broiled until it's sticky and golden. It comes together in about twenty minutes, which makes it a proper weeknight winner.

## Ingredients

- 4 salmon fillets (6 oz / 170 g each)
- ¼ cup mayonnaise (or Greek yogurt for a tangier, lighter option)
- 2 tbsp sriracha (or sweet chili sauce for milder heat)
- 1 tbsp honey (or maple syrup)
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced

## Method

1. Preheat your oven's broiler to high. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper for easy cleanup.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, sriracha, honey, rice vinegar, and soy sauce. Stir in the sesame oil.
3. Place the salmon fillets on the prepared baking sheet. Generously brush them with the bang bang sauce, coating both sides for maximum flavour.
4. Broil the salmon for 8–10 minutes, monitoring closely to prevent burning. The fillets are ready when they flake easily with a fork.
5. Garnish with the sliced green onions and serve immediately.

## Notes

Make-ahead: the bang bang sauce keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for up to a week — just give it a good stir before using.

Air fryer option: set it to 400°F (200°C) and cook for 8–10 minutes for crispy results.

To customise: add a squeeze of lime juice for extra brightness, or scatter sesame seeds on top for crunch.
