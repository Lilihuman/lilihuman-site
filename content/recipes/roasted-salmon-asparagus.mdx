Adapted from a Mealime recipe. This is the weeknight dinner that looks like you fussed — salmon and asparagus roast together on one sheet pan, then get spooned with a quick browned-butter and balsamic sauce. Thirty minutes, minimal washing up.

## Ingredients

- 0.68 kg salmon fillet
- 2 small bunches asparagus
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp + 2 tsp extra virgin olive oil (divided)
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp salt (divided)
- 1 tsp black pepper (divided)

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F) and position a rack in the centre. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Peel and mince the garlic.
3. Wash the asparagus and snap (or cut) off the woody ends. Transfer to the baking sheet, drizzle with 2 tbsp olive oil, and season with the minced garlic, ½ tsp salt, and ½ tsp black pepper; toss to coat. Spread out in a single layer, leaving room for the salmon in the middle.
4. Place the salmon fillets skin-side down in the middle of the baking sheet. Rub with the remaining 2 tsp olive oil and season with ½ tsp salt and ½ tsp black pepper.
5. Place in the oven and bake until the salmon is opaque and the asparagus is tender, 10–15 minutes.
6. Meanwhile, in a small saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat. Let it brown slightly, then remove from the heat. Stir in the balsamic vinegar and soy sauce. Cover to keep warm.
7. To serve, arrange the asparagus on a plate and place the salmon on top. Spoon the balsamic-butter sauce over the top and enjoy.

## Notes

Browning the butter is worth the extra minute — it gives the sauce a nutty depth that balances the sharp balsamic. Serve with rice or crusty bread to catch every drop.
