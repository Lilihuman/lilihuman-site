A recipe from Estelle Human. These are the sausage rolls that disappear first at any gathering — a warmly spiced beef filling in crisp, golden pastry.

## Ingredients

- 1 onion, chopped or grated
- 500 g ground beef
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 3 cups flour (for the pastry, or use ready-made puff pastry)
- 2 eggs (1 for the filling, 1 beaten for egg wash)

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C) and line a baking tray.
2. In a bowl, combine the ground beef, onion, cumin, coriander, turmeric, salt, pepper, and 1 egg. Mix well until evenly seasoned.
3. Roll out your pastry into long rectangles. Spoon the beef filling in a line down the centre of each.
4. Fold the pastry over the filling and seal the edge, then cut into individual rolls.
5. Place seam-side down on the tray and brush the tops with the beaten egg.
6. Bake for approximately 30–40 minutes, until the pastry is golden and the filling is cooked through.

## Notes

Ready-rolled puff pastry makes these quick for a weeknight; homemade shortcrust from the flour gives a sturdier, more traditional roll. Serve warm with chutney or tomato sauce.
