Some nights you just need something good on the table fast. This is that dinner — three ingredients, a little salt and pepper, and one pan. It's become my go-to when I want something wholesome without the effort.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (450 g) ground turkey
- 1 head broccoli, diced (about 4 cups)
- ⅓ cup sweet Thai chili sauce
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground turkey, season with salt and pepper, and cook, breaking it up, until browned and cooked through, 6–8 minutes.
2. Add the diced broccoli (and a splash of water), cover, and cook until tender-crisp, 4–5 minutes.
3. Stir in the sweet Thai chili sauce and toss to coat. Cook for another 1–2 minutes until everything is glossy and warmed through. Divide into bowls and serve.

## Notes

Great for meal prep — it keeps well in the fridge for a few days and reheats beautifully. Serve over rice or cauliflower rice if you'd like to stretch it further.
