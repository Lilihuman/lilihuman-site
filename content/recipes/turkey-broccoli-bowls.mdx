Some nights you just need something good on the table fast. This is that dinner — three ingredients, a little salt and pepper, and one pan. It's become my go-to when I want something wholesome without the effort.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (450 g) ground turkey
- 1 head broccoli, diced (about 4 cups)
- Sweet Thai chili sauce, to taste
- Salt and pepper, or your choice of seasoning
- Cooked rice or quinoa, to serve

## Method

1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and cook the ground turkey thoroughly, breaking it up as it browns. Season with your choice of seasoning, or just salt and pepper.
2. Add the diced broccoli and cook slightly, so it stays crunchy.
3. Stir in as much of the sweet Thai chili sauce as you desire and toss to coat.
4. Serve over rice or quinoa.

## Notes

Great for meal prep — it keeps well in the fridge for a few days and reheats beautifully.
