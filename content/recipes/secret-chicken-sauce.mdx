A recipe from Estelle Human. This is the tray of chicken that quietly steals the show — the sauce caramelises into something sticky and golden, and there's never any left.

## Ingredients

- 8 chicken thighs
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1 cup chutney
- 1 cup hot water
- 1 pack onion soup
- 2 tbsp apricot jam
- 3 tsp salt and pepper

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C).
2. Mix the mayonnaise, chutney, hot water, onion soup, apricot jam, and salt and pepper into a sauce.
3. Cover the chicken with the sauce, separating the thighs to make sure the sauce gets through and around each piece.
4. Cover with foil, shiny side facing towards the chicken.
5. Bake for 30 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for a further hour, until sticky and golden.

## Notes

Serve with rice or mashed potato to soak up all that sauce. It only gets better as leftovers the next day.
