A recipe from Estelle Human. Milk tart is one of those desserts that tastes like home, and this microwave method means you can have it done in about twenty minutes — no fussing over a stovetop.

## Ingredients

**For boiling:**

- 2 cups milk
- 1 tbsp butter
- Cinnamon, for sprinkling

**For whisking:**

- 2 eggs
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 tbsp flour
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- ½ tsp vanilla
- Pinch of salt

**To finish:**

- Sweetened tart shells

## Method

1. Put the milk and butter in a microwave-safe jug and bring to a boil in the microwave, 1 minute at a time (approximately 3–4 minutes total).
2. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, sugar, flour, cornstarch, vanilla, and salt until smooth.
3. Pour the boiled milk into the bowl and mix well.
4. Microwave again, 1.5 minutes at a time (approximately 4 minutes total), until thickened.
5. Scoop into the sweetened tart shells and sprinkle cinnamon on top.

## Notes

Serve chilled or at room temperature. The custard sets further as it cools, so don't worry if it looks a little soft coming out of the microwave.
